# ⭐ NOVA BRAWL (נובה ברול: ארקייד הכוח)

🚀 **Play Live on Vercel:** **[https://nova-brawl.vercel.app](https://nova-brawl.vercel.app)**

**Brawl-Stars-inspired Bilingual (Hebrew RTL default + English LTR) Action Arcade & Interactive Math Hacker Codex** — tailored for 9-year-olds who love **Kirby, Mario, Sonic, Geometry Dash, Brawl Stars, Dragons, Dinosaurs, Spaceships, and Robots**.

Built with **intrinsic math mechanics**, **zero-anxiety Bullet-Time**, **physical place-value & array manipulatives**, and a complete **12-Card Secret Gamer Strategy Deck & Smart 10×10 Multiplication Matrix** designed specifically for ADHD & ASD learners.

---

## 🎮 4 Visually & Mechanically Distinct Arcade Worlds

1. **🦖 Mecha-Dino Forge (זירת הדינו-רובוט) — Addition & Core Fusion (`+`):**
   - *Kirby / Mega-Man style absorption:* Inhale floating **Tens Bars (`+10`, `+20`, `+30`)** and **Ones Sparks (`+1`..`+9`)** into your Fusion Reactor, or hit **⚡ Split into Tens & Ones** to break 2-digit numbers apart visually before firing a Mega-Blast at Mecha-Rex 9000!
2. **🚀 Galactic Star-Slicer (גלקסיית החלליות) — Subtraction & Warp Number-Bridge (`-`):**
   - *Space shooter & number-bridge jumper:* Hop across the gap from `b` up to `a` (`82 − 67 ➔ 67 + 3 = 70, + 12 = 82 ➔ 15`) or fire the **🛡️ Equal Shift Beam (`+1/+1`)** to turn nasty borrows like `73 − 39` into an effortless `74 − 40 = 34`!
3. **🐉 Cyber-Dragon Multi-Storm (דרקון הסייבר: סופת הכפל) — Multiplication & Laser Grid Slicer (`×`):**
   - *Emberwing / Boss Swarm Barrage:* Enemies fly in **living 2D geometric drone formations (`rows × cols`)**. Click between any two rows to **slice a tough multiplication grid in two with your Dragon Laser Claw** (e.g., slicing `7 × 8` into `5×8=40` in gold + `2×8=16` in cyan = `56`), or click **🔄 Mirror Flip** to rotate the swarm 90°!
4. **⚡ Neon Dash: Rhythm Runner (ניאון דאש: ריצת הקצב) — 3-Lane Portal Dash + Smart Bullet-Time (`+ / - / ×`):**
   - *Geometry Dash / Sonic style 60fps synthwave highway:* Dash across 3 neon lanes. As you approach the Triple Energy Portals, **Smart Bullet-Time (זמן קליע חכם)** smoothly slows down time so there is **zero timer panic**, then blasts off with a sonic boom as soon as you lock in!

## 📓 The Hacker's Math Codex & Secret Cheat Sheet (ספר ההאקים הסודי)

Accessible anytime from the top bar:
- **🧮 Smart 10×10 Multiplication Matrix:** Color-coded by trick family, featuring a 1-click **🪞 Mirror Law Filter** (hides the redundant half of the table) and a **💎 Final Boss Filter** (proving that once you know `×1, ×2, ×5, ×9, ×10` and Mirror Flip, only **6 core facts** remain!).
- **⚡ Live Hack Simulator:** Pick or slide *any* two numbers for `+`, `-`, or `×` and watch the visual manipulative diagram (10-Finger Cyber Hand for `×9`, Equal-Shift Rails, Ninja Number-Bridge, or Sliced Drone Array) break it down step by step.
- **🃏 12 Pro Gamer Strategy Cards:** Bite-sized visual cards teaching *Magnet-10*, *Tens-First Split*, *Friendly-10 Swap*, *Twin Clone +1*, *Equal Shift (Zero Borrow)*, *Ninja Bridge Jump*, *Double-Double (`×4, ×8`)*, *Half-of-10 (`×5`)*, *Cyber-9 Finger Scanner*, and *5-6-7-8 (`56=7×8`)*.
- **🖨️ Printable 1-Page Pocket Cheat Sheet:** Ready to print or keep open during homework.

## 🧠 Neurodivergent-First (ADHD & ASD) Design Details

- **Hebrew Default (`lang="he" dir="rtl"`) + Instant English Toggle (`EN`):** Styled after *Brawl Stars* with `Secular One` / `Rubik` and `Lilita One` / `Nunito`, while isolating math expressions in `dir="ltr"` containers so equations never flip backward.
- **No Punitive Streak Resets:** Shield deflections never wipe out progress; instead they automatically open the **Smart Hack Visor** and award **+25 Tactical Hack XP** for learning the shortcut.
- **🌿 Calm Mode (מצב רגוע):** 1-click sensory regulation toggle in the top bar that disables screen shake, confetti bursts, and rapid animations.
