# NOVA BRAWL (נובה ברול: ארקייד הכוח)
## Research, Game Design & Neurodivergent Math Pedagogy Blueprint

### 1. Executive Summary & The Problem with "Edutainment"
A bright 9-year-old boy who loves **Kirby, Mario, Sonic, Geometry Dash, Brawl Stars, Dragons, Dinosaurs, Spaceships, and Robots**—and who has **ADHD and light ASD**—will immediately spot and reject traditional "math games" (extrinsic math gates).

#### Why Traditional Math Games Fail Neurodivergent Kids:
1. **Extrinsic "Math Gates" Break Flow:** Stopping a game to pop up a flashcard (`47 + 35 = ?`) feels like a chore disguised as a game.
2. **Working Memory Overload:** Two-digit mental addition (`47 + 35`) or subtraction with borrowing (`82 - 67`, `73 - 39`) requires holding multiple intermediate digits in working memory—the exact cognitive bottleneck associated with ADHD.
3. **Timer Anxiety & Rejection Sensitive Dysphoria (RSD):** Ticking countdown clocks trigger fight-or-flight freeze responses, while harsh "WRONG! Streak Reset to 0" screens cause frustration and disengagement.
4. **Rote Memorization vs. Pattern Systems:** Kids on the spectrum and with ADHD thrive when shown the **underlying system ("Hacks" / "Glitches" / "Cheat Codes")** rather than being told to memorize 100 isolated facts.

### 2. Core Design Pillars of NOVA BRAWL

#### Pillar A: Intrinsic Math Mechanics (The Math IS the Weapon)
Instead of answering a quiz to earn a turn, **numbers are physical energy cores, laser frequencies, drone formations, and warp-jumps**:
- **Addition = Fusion & Absorption (Kirby / Mega-Man Style):** In **World 1: Mecha-Dino Forge (זירת הדינו-רובוט)**, you control a hero who physically inhales/absorbs floating **Tens Bars (`+10`, `+20`, `+30`)** and **Ones Sparks (`+1`..`+9`)** into a visual Fusion Reactor until your reactor matches the Mecha-Dino's Armor Frequency, then unleashes a **MEGA-BLAST**.
- **Subtraction = Laser Slicing & Warp-Bridge Jumping (Space Shooter Style):** In **World 2: Galactic Star-Slicer (גלקסיית החלליות)**, you pilot the Nova Viper spaceship. You either carve down an Alien Mothership's Overcharged Shield using `-10` and `-1` Slicer Torpedoes, OR switch to **Warp-Bridge Jump Mode** (counting up from `67 → 70 → 82` in two easy hops: `+3` and `+12` = `15`).
- **Multiplication = Living Drone Arrays & Laser Grid Slicing (Emberwing / Boss Barrage Style):** In **World 3: Cyber-Dragon Multi-Storm (דרקון הסייבר: סופת הכפל)**, enemies appear in **living 2D geometric formations** (e.g., `7 rows × 8 drones`). You can use the **Dragon Claw Laser Slicer** to physically slice a hard `7 × 8` swarm into two friendly swarms: **`5 × 8 (40)`** + **`2 × 8 (16)`** = **`56`**!
- **Speed & Flow = Bullet-Time Portal Runner (Geometry Dash / Sonic Style):** In **World 4: Neon Dash Runner (ניאון דאש: ריצת הקצב)**, your hero dashes down a 3-lane synthwave highway toward Triple Energy Portals. As you approach a portal gate, **Smart Bullet-Time (זמן קליע חכם)** smoothly slows down time so you get all the adrenaline of Sonic/Geometry Dash with **zero timer panic**.

#### Pillar B: "Hacker Glitch Chips" (טריקים והאקים של גיימרים) Inside Combat
In every single level, the player has a **HACK VISOR (האק חכם ⚡)** button that doesn't penalize them—in fact, using it awards **"+25 TACTICAL HACK XP!"** because using smart mental strategies *is* the goal!
Pressing **HACK** physically transforms the problem on screen:
- Turns `58 + 25` into `60 + 23 = 83` (Friendly-10 Magnet) or `(50+20) + (8+5) = 70 + 13 = 83` (Tens/Ones Split).
- Turns `73 - 39` into `74 - 40 = 34` (Equal Shift `+1` to both numbers—eliminating borrowing completely!).
- Turns `82 - 67` into a visual 2-step bridge: `67 --(+3)--> 70 --(+12)--> 82` (`3 + 12 = 15`).
- Turns `9 × 7` into an interactive 10-Finger Cyber Hand with finger #7 folded (`6` tens on the left, `3` ones on the right = `63`).

#### Pillar C: Brawl Stars Bilingual UI (Hebrew Default RTL + English LTR)
- **Extruded Tactile Slabs:** Inspired by Supercell's *Brawl Stars* and Dror's *Fluid Lab* / *Cloud Bridge Racers*: thick dark outlines, vibrant cobalt/gold/magenta/cyan slabs that physically depress on click, bold typography (`Rubik` & `Secular One` for Hebrew, `Lilita One` & `Nunito` for English).
- **Hebrew RTL with LTR Math Isolation:** While all UI navigation, progress bars, and story prompts flow naturally in Hebrew (`dir="rtl"`), all mathematical equations and number lines are isolated in `dir="ltr"` containers so `73 - 39 = 34` never flips backward.
- **Sensory Regulation (Calm Mode):** A 1-click **Calm Mode (מצב רגוע 🌿)** toggle in the top bar reduces screen shake, particle flashes, and high-frequency visual noise for ASD/ADHD sensory comfort.

### 3. The 12 Tailored Math Hacks & Cheat Sheet Curriculum

| # | Operation | Strategy Name (EN) | Strategy Name (HE) | How the Visual Trick Works |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `+` (1-2d) | **Magnet-10 Partners** | **מגנט העשר (חברים ל-10)** | Pair digits that sum to 10 (`9+1, 8+2, 7+3, 6+4, 5+5`). For `8 + 5`, pull `2` from `5` to lock `10 + 3 = 13`. |
| 2 | `+` (2d) | **Tens-First Laser Split** | **פיצול לייזר: עשרות ואז אחדות** | For `47 + 35`: Fuse Tens (`40 + 30 = 70`), Fuse Ones (`7 + 5 = 12`), Merge (`70 + 12 = 82`). No right-to-left carrying stress! |
| 3 | `+` (2d) | **Friendly-10 Turbo Swap** | **קפיצה לעשרת עגולה** | For `58 + 25`: Give `2` from `25` to `58` → becomes `60 + 23 = 83`! |
| 4 | `+` (1-2d) | **Twin Clone +1** | **שיבוט תאומים (+1)** | Know doubles (`6+6=12, 7+7=14, 25+25=50`)? Then `6+7` is just `6+6+1=13`, and `25+26` is `50+1=51`. |
| 5 | `-` (2d) | **Ninja Bridge Jump (Count Up)** | **גשר הקפיצות (סופרים קדימה!)** | For `82 - 67`: Don't subtract! Start at `67`, jump `+3` to `70`, jump `+12` to `82`. Add the jumps: `3 + 12 = 15`. |
| 6 | `-` (2d) | **Equal Shift Shield (No Borrow!)** | **הזזה שווה (בלי פריטה!)** | For `73 - 39`: Add `+1` to BOTH numbers so the second ends in zero: `74 - 40 = 34`! Works for `-8` (`+2`) and `-7` (`+3`) too! |
| 7 | `-` (2d) | **Two-Step Safe Slice** | **חיתוך בשני שלבים** | For `64 - 28`: Subtract tens first (`64 - 20 = 44`), then subtract `4` to hit `40`, then the remaining `4` → `36`. |
| 8 | `×` (1d) | **Mirror Flip Law** | **חוק המראה (חצי לוח במתנה!)** | `7 × 3` is identical to `3 × 7 = 21`. Slices the 10×10 multiplication table in half! |
| 9 | `×` (1d) | **Double, Double-Double, Triple-Double** | **הכפלה כפולה ומשולשת (`×2, ×4, ×8`)** | `×2` = Double. `×4` = Double twice (`4×7`: `7→14→28`). `×8` = Double 3 times (`8×6`: `6→12→24→48`). |
| 10 | `×` (1d) | **Half-and-Zero (`×5`)** | **טריק החצי-אפס (`×5`)** | Multiply by 10, cut in half! `5 × 8` → half of `8` is `4`, add `0` → `40`. For odd: `5 × 7` = `5×6 (30) + 5 = 35`. |
| 11 | `×` (1d) | **Cyber-Hands 9x Scanner** | **סורק אצבעות הניאון (`×9`)** | Hold 10 fingers. For `9 × 4`, fold finger #4: `3` fingers left (`30`) + `6` fingers right (`6`) = `36`. Also: `10×n - n` (`40 - 4 = 36`), and digits always sum to `9`! |
| 12 | `×` (1d) | **Boss Squares & 5-6-7-8 Code** | **אלכסון הריבועים וקוד 5-6-7-8** | Use `5×n` + `2×n` for `7×n`, rhyme `6 × 8 = 48`, and secret code `5, 6, 7, 8` → `56 = 7 × 8`! |
